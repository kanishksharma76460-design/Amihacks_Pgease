## 1. GST on PG and hostel accommodation

**Sources:** TaxGuru (Adv. Satyajit Naik), 18 Aug 2025 `[VERIFIED]`; ClearTax, 19 Jun 2025 `[VERIFIED]`

### 1.1 The core rule

PG/hostel is classified as **commercial lodging under SAC 9963**.

| Scenario | GST rate |
|---|---|
| PG / hostel (post-18 Jul 2022) | **12%** |
| **Stay ≥ 90 days AND rent ≤ ₹20,000/person/month** | **NIL** (Entry 12AA) |
| Hostel run by an educational institution | **NIL** |
| Guest house ≤ ₹1,000/day | Nil |
| Guest house ₹1,000–7,500/day | 12% |
| Guest house > ₹7,500/day | 18% |

### 1.2 The exemption — Entry 12AA

**Notification 12/2017-Central Tax (Rate), Entry 12AA, effective 15 July 2024.**
Exempt if **BOTH** conditions hold:
1. Monthly rent **≤ ₹20,000 per person**, AND
2. Continuous stay **≥ 90 days**

**Retrospective** application from **1 July 2017** to 14 July 2024 per
**Circular No. 228/22/2024-GST**. **No sunset clause** on refund claims.

Operators eligible for the exemption **need not register under GST**, and **RCM does not apply**.

### 1.3 ⚠️ The bundling trap — this is the key commercial risk

**Bundling food, laundry or internet can push the total value above ₹20,000 —
which destroys the exemption, even for long stays.**

This is a **product-design constraint, not just a tax note.** Any platform that helps owners
package services must keep the *rent component* and the *service component* separately
disclosed, or it will inadvertently push owners into a 12% GST liability. **That is a
revenue-killing outcome for a small owner, and a trust-killing outcome for the platform.**

### 1.4 Judicial support

- ***Thai Mookambikaa Ladies Hostel v. Union of India*** (Madras HC, 2024) — hostels for girl
  students and working women are **residential dwellings**, exempt under Entry 12
- ***Taghar Vasudeva Ambrish*** (Karnataka HC, 2022)
- Maharashtra AAR (2024), for educational-society hostels

### 1.5 ⚠️ Informal-sector abuse pattern

TaxGuru documents a **viral Bengaluru case (August 2025)** where a PG **demanded cash-only rent
and threatened a 12% GST surcharge for digital payments**.

**This is a direct design requirement:** a platform that pushes digital payment will be resisted
by owners who use "GST" as a pretext to demand cash. **Neutralising that objection — by showing
the owner they actually owe *zero* GST under Entry 12AA — is a concrete, high-value feature.**

### 1.6 Women's hostels

⚠️ **No special GST exemption applies.** Same rules for all. ClearTax states this explicitly.

## 2. Licensing — GHMC

### 2.1 Fire NOC

**Source:** GHMC official portal, retrieved 22 Sep 2026 `[VERIFIED]`

**Process:** Online application via **eghmc.ghmc.gov.in** → District Fire Officer dashboard →
assigned to Zonal Station Fire Officer → **physical inspection** → report online → District Fire
Officer scrutiny → Additional Director Fire Services → **Chief City Planner** →
**Commissioner, GHMC** approves/rejects → downloadable certificate.
Applicant receives **SMS status updates** at each stage.

**Documents required:** Site plan · basement floor plan · stilt/ground floor plan · floor-wise
plan · terrace plan · section plan · elevation plan · provisional/revised provisional NOC
(prerequisite for occupancy NOC)

**Service-level timelines:**

| Building height | SLA |
|---|---|
| Under 15 m | **7 days** |
| Above 15 m | **14 days** |

## 3. ⚠️ Project Safe Stay / Suraksha Nivas 1.0 — the biggest regulatory event

**The most important recent development in this market.** It converts hostel operation from an
informal business into a regulated one with hard deadlines.

### 3.1 The audit

**Source:** NewsMeter, 18 Jul 2026 `[VERIFIED]`

- **10 special teams**, **447 women's hostels**, over **9 weekends**
- Conducted in the **Hyderabad Police Commissionerate** alone

**Findings:**

| Finding | Value |
|---|---|
| Had CCTV | 97.5% |
| — of which **non-functional** | **~30%** |
| Had proper **compound walls** | **43%** |
| Had **fire safety equipment** | **38.9%** |
| Had a **fire exit plan** | **0%** |
| Scored **below 40/100** overall | **62%** |
| Had valid **GHMC trade licence** | **44%** |

### 3.2 New mandates

- **Mandatory police registration via Form-A** at the local police station — for all women's
  hostels and PGs in the Hyderabad Commissionerate
- **24/7 security guards** at all entry/exit points
- **Background verification** of housekeeping, support and kitchen staff
- **GHMC Trade Licence mandatory** — "large-scale hostels are commercial establishments"
- **FSSAI licence mandatory** if food is served; otherwise "unauthorised." **Criminal cases
  possible** for unsafe/substandard/misbranded food
- **Second staircase** for emergency evacuation
- **2-kg fire extinguishers** on every floor **and inside every room**
- **Hose reel system** connected to rooftop water tanks
- **"Safe Bathrooms"** concept to eliminate privacy violations
- **2-month rectification window** (from 18 Jul 2026 — **this deadline has now passed**)
- A **dedicated mobile app** for women's hostel safety announced; **Suraksha Nivas 2.0** to follow

### 3.3 Key quotes and named officials

> "The objective is **not to burden hostel operators**, but to establish minimum safety standards
> and create a secure living environment for women."
> — Police Commissioner **V.C. Sajjanar**

**Named officials (useful for citation):** Police Commissioner **V.C. Sajjanar** ·
GHMC Commissioner **R.V. Karnan** · District Fire Officer **T. Venkanna** ·
Assistant Food Controller **P. Murthy** · HCSC Secretary General **Shekhar Reddy**

**Partner bodies:** Hyderabad City Police + GHMC + **Hyderabad City Security Council (HCSC)** +
United Way of Hyderabad

**Framing** `[VERIFIED — Hyderabad Stories, 20 Jul 2026]`: hostels are being shifted from
"a casual business to a responsible community service"; hostels "are legally classified as
commercial entities."

### 3.4 ⚠️ Timescale warning

**The 2-month compliance window closed around 18 September 2026 — four days before this
compilation.** The first enforcement tranche may have already produced outcomes. **Re-search
for enforcement actions before proceeding.**

## 4. Prior enforcement precedent

**Source:** NewsMeter, 24 May 2025 `[VERIFIED]` — GHMC Special Task Force

**Action:** **58 hostels** inspected (23 May 2025) in **Ameerpet, Ashok Nagar, Dilsukhnagar**;
**30 notices** · **5 kitchens sealed** · **₹2,45,500** penalties under the **GHMC Act, 1955**

**Violations documented — highly citable, and directly relevant to the food problem:**

- **Cooking areas located in cellars and parking spaces** — against urban planning norms
- Food prepared in **unhygienic conditions**; kitchens **without ventilation**
- **Greasy exhaust systems**; unclean ceilings and walls
- **Housefly infestations** and **uncovered dustbins** in food areas
- **No gloves or hairnets** among food handlers
- **Overcrowding and unsanitary toilets — some opening directly into kitchen spaces**
- **Water stagnation and uneven flooring**
- **Operating without valid FSSAI and trade licences**

**This is the strongest single evidence block for pre-2026 regulatory non-compliance.**

## 5. The CURE Bill — replacing the GHMC Act 1955

**Source:** NewsMeter, 6 Jul 2026 `[VERIFIED]`

**What it is:** **Draft Core Urban Region (Integrated Governance) Bill, 2026** — replaces the
**GHMC Act, 1955**

**Scope:** brings **GHMC, Cyberabad and Malkajgiri** municipal corporations under an integrated
governance framework

### 5.1 PG-relevant provisions

- **"Simplified building approval processes and a single trade licence system"** aimed at
  improving ease of doing business
- **CURE Apex Governance Council** coordinating civic agencies
- **SMART Governance Centre** for real-time monitoring
- **Unified digital platform** for civic services
- **GIS-based urban planning**
- Integrated underground utility management
- ⚠️ **Property tax shift: from Annual Rental Value (ARV) → Capital Value-based assessment** —
  this **materially affects commercial-property and PG tax economics**
- Incentives for sustainable buildings; rebates for timely property-tax payment

**Process:** Public consultation open until **24 July 2026**; feedback via GHMC website;
RWAs, professionals, industry bodies and civil society invited.

### 5.2 Related developments `[SNIPPET]`

- **GO Ms.No.55 dated 11.02.2026** — Reorganisation of Core Urban Region: constitution of
  **3 Municipal Corporations** (Cyberabad, Malkajgiri, Greater Hyderabad),
  Metropolitan Area & Urban Development (GHMC-1) Department
- **Telangana Assembly passed the CURE Bill**, replacing the 1955 Act (The News Minute)
- **Telangana Ordinance 9 of 2025**: having **7 Municipal Corporations** in the Core Urban Region
  under the Telangana Municipalities Act 2019 alongside GHMC under the 1955 Act "has resulted in
  **administrative fragmentation… uneven service delivery**"

### 5.3 ⚠️ How it affects PG licensing — inference, not fact

The Bill's **"single trade licence system"** is the key mechanism. Today a PG in Cyberabad vs
GHMC territory may face different licensing paths; CURE would unify them.

**No source explicitly states the effect on PG licensing.** `[UNVERIFIED as a specific claim]`

## 6. Rent agreements, stamp duty & e-stamping

**Source:** MagicBricks, 1 Sep 2025 `[VERIFIED]`

| Item | Rate |
|---|---|
| **Stamp duty** (residential, up to 11 months) | **0.5% of total annual rent** |
| **Registration fee** | **0.2% of total annual rent**; min **₹1,000** |
| Commercial rent agreements | **Higher** |

**Worked example from source:** annual rent ₹2,40,000 → stamp duty **₹1,200**

### 6.1 Rules

- Agreements **longer than 11 months MUST be registered** at the Sub-Registrar's office
- **Online/digital rent agreements are valid** in Telangana
- **Notarisation not mandatory** if registered, but adds legal strength; needed for
  high-value/commercial
- **E-stamping accepted for all agreements**; physical stamp papers "getting phased out,
  especially post-July 2025"; via **SHCIL** or an authorised provider
- **Both parties must sign every page**; witnesses required
- ⚠️ **The security deposit amount must be stated in the document**

**Official portals:** `registration.telangana.gov.in` (IGRS Telangana);
Ready Reckoner under **G.O.Ms.No.59, dt: 20.07.2021**

### 6.2 Why this matters operationally

This defines the **paperwork cost floor**. And note the implication from §4: most PGs were found
**operating without valid licences** — which strongly suggests **most PGs also operate without
registered agreements.** That is both a compliance risk for owners and a **product opportunity**
(digital, e-stamped agreements).

## 7. Model Tenancy Act 2021 — Telangana status

⚠️ **UNRESOLVED — sources directly contradict each other.**

| Source | Claim |
|---|---|
| Hindustan Times (Jun 2022) | Only **4 states** revised laws on MTA lines: AP, UP, Maharashtra, Assam `[SNIPPET]` |
| RegNews | "AP adopted the act, **Telangana not yet**" `[SNIPPET]` |
| commoner-law.com (2026) | In Telangana, MTA's deposit cap, 90-day rent-hike notice, and Rent Authority/Court/Tribunal structure **"do not apply here"** `[SNIPPET]` |
| prorently.in | Claims Telangana **has** enacted `[SNIPPET]` — ⚠️ **contradicts the other three; judged unreliable** |
| **Centre's position** | MoHUA formulated MTA "to balance the rights of tenants and landlords and provide a speedy dispute resolution mechanism" — **PIB, 11 Dec 2025**. ⚠️ **The PIB release does not list adopting states.** `[VERIFIED]` |
| sansad.in (USQ 1745) | Contains an official list of adopting states — **could not be extracted** `[UNVERIFIED]` |

**Working conclusion: Telangana has likely NOT adopted the Model Tenancy Act.**
Treat as "likely, not confirmed." **The primary official list (sansad.in) must be read before
publishing anything that depends on this.**

**Why it matters:** if MTA does not apply, there is **no statutory deposit cap, no mandated
90-day rent-hike notice, and no Rent Authority/Tribunal** in Telangana. That explains the
deposit chaos observed in [`01-pg-market.md`](01-pg-market.md) §3.4 — **there is no law setting
a norm.**

## 8. Regulatory summary for product design

| Requirement | Status | Product implication |
|---|---|---|
| GST ≤₹20,000 + ≥90 days = NIL | **In force** (Entry 12AA, Jul 2024) | **Feature:** calculate GST liability per tenant; warn before bundling pushes over the threshold |
| GHMC trade licence | **Mandatory**; 44% compliance | **Feature:** licence status tracking + renewal reminders |
| Fire NOC | **Mandatory**; 7–14 day SLA | **Feature:** document vault with expiry alerts |
| Form-A police registration | **Mandatory** (women's hostels, Hyderabad) | **Feature:** guided registration workflow |
| FSSAI (if serving food) | **Mandatory**; criminal liability for unsafe food | **Feature:** food-provider compliance tracking |
| 24/7 guards, 2nd staircase, per-room extinguishers, hose reel | **Mandatory** | **This is a capital cost shock** — owners will resist. Opportunity: financing/partner referrals |
| Registered rent agreement (>11 months) | **Mandatory** | **Feature:** digital e-stamped agreements |
| Property tax ARV → capital value | **Pending (CURE Bill)** | Watch item — changes owner economics |
| Model Tenancy Act | **Not adopted (likely)** | **No legal deposit standard exists** → an explicit, transparent deposit policy is a differentiator |
