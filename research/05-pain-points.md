### A3. Deposit and money pain points

**Observed deposit chaos** `[VERIFIED — NoBroker listings, 22 Sep 2026]`:
Same micro-market, same day — rent ₹7,000 → deposit ₹2,000 (0.29×);
rent ₹7,500 → deposit ₹10,500 (**1.40×**); rent ₹5,000 → deposit ₹10,000 (**2.00×**).

**Root cause is legal, not behavioural:** ⚠️ **Telangana has likely not adopted the Model Tenancy
Act**, so there is **no statutory deposit cap and no Rent Authority/Tribunal**
(see [`04-regulations.md`](04-regulations.md) §7). **There is no law setting a norm.**

**This is a documented, verified owner cost shock** — and it arrives on owners running

## PART D — Technology adoption gap

### D1. What owners actually use today

**The informal baseline is: registers + WhatsApp + cash.**
Sourced only from vendor marketing `[VERIFIED page, unverified claim]` — but consistent with the
regulatory evidence (see below).

### D2. Proxy indicators that are `[VERIFIED]` and reliable

1. **Pricing signals the gap.** PMS products price at **₹25/bed/month** and **₹159/month flat** —
   extremely low. Consistent with software being a discretionary add-on, **not** mission-critical.
2. **WhatsApp is the primary channel.** PGManage markets "Collect rent on WhatsApp";
   the JumboTiger testimonial cites "WhatsApp groups."
   → **In this market, WhatsApp is the incumbent operating system, not a channel to be added later.**
3. **Enforcement is physical, not digital.** GHMC and Police rely on **physical raids and audits**
   (58 hostels; 447 hostels) rather than digital registers —
   **direct evidence that digital record-keeping is not the norm.**
4. **The new regime may force adoption.** Suraksha Nivas requires "complete records of residents,"
   functional CCTV, staff background verification, and a forthcoming **mobile app for monitoring**.
   **This is the first genuine regulatory pull for digitisation in this market.**

| Pain | Severity | Evidence | Who can act | Addressable by software? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Fire safety / licensing non-compliance** | 🔴 Critical | **Tier 1 (regulator)** | Owner | Partly — document tracking, reminders |

| **Fake reviews / photos** | 🟠 High | Tier 4 only | Platform | **Yes** — verification |

| **Inflated electricity bills** | 🟠 Medium | Tier 4 only | Platform | **Yes** — metered billing |
| **GST confusion / cash coercion** | 🟠 Medium | **Tier 2 (TaxGuru)** | Platform | **Yes** — GST calculator |
| **Curfews** | 🟡 Medium | **Tier 3** | Owner | No — it's a feature incumbents sell |

**The three highest-value, best-evidenced software-addressable problems:**

3. **Airport transfer / tourist transport** (446 documented cases, directly monetisable)
