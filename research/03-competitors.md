### 3.1 Why this competitive set is weak (and what it tells us)

**Pricing signals the gap.** ₹25/bed/month and ₹159/month flat are **extremely low price points**.
That is consistent with a market where software is a discretionary add-on and where vendors
cannot extract value — **not** with a market where software is mission-critical.

**WhatsApp is the primary channel.** PGManage markets "Collect rent on WhatsApp"; JumboTiger's
client testimonial describes "WhatsApp groups." **In this market, WhatsApp is the incumbent
operating system**, not a channel to be added later.

**Enforcement is physical, not digital.** GHMC and Police rely on **physical raids and audits**
(58 hostels; 447 hostels) — direct evidence that **digital record-keeping is not the norm**.

**But the regulatory pull is new.** Suraksha Nivas requires "complete records of residents,"
functional CCTV, staff background verification, and a **forthcoming mobile app for monitoring**.
**This is the first genuine regulatory mandate for digitisation in this market.**

## 5. Competitive summary

| Layer | Competitive intensity | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| **Co-living operators (beds)** | Medium — ~5 funded players, consolidating | Capital-intensive; Stanza and Colive/Bain/Sattva are the ones to watch. **Hard to enter.** |
| **Listings marketplaces** | High — MagicBricks, NoBroker, 99acres, Housing all present | ⚠️ **NestAway's $220M → ₹90Cr collapse shows this is a graveyard.** Avoid competing head-on on listings. |
| **PMS / owner tooling** | **Low** — fragmented, tiny vendors, ₹25–159/month pricing | **The gap.** But the low willingness-to-pay is a real constraint. |
| **Tourism discovery / booking** | Very high — MakeMyTrip at 53.8% OTA share | ⚠️ **Do not compete with MMT on flights/hotels.** |
| **Hyderabad-specific PG + tourism bridge** | **Empty** | See [`06-product-opportunity.md`](06-product-opportunity.md) |

### The strategic conclusion

**Don't be a marketplace. Don't be an operator. Be the operating layer.**

The operators need beds and occupancy. The marketplaces have failed on unit economics. The PMS
vendors are too small and priced too low to fund a real product. **Nothing connects the two
markets — accommodation supply and tourism demand.**

That bridge is the opening. It is detailed in [`06-product-opportunity.md`](06-product-opportunity.md).
